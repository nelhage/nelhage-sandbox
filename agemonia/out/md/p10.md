### Ability Checks (p6)

An ability check may be either active or reactive. Active ability checks are used when an Ally is actively trying to achieve a task or resist certain effects. Reactive ability checks are used when an Ally is trying to react to something, such as resisting damage from an Enemy attack.

#### Active Ability Checks [Action Die] (p6)

An active ability check is signified by a number in a red box preceded by one of the 3 ability symbols. An active ability check is performed by rolling a number of [Action Die] equal to your Ally's level in the given ability. The ability symbol displayed in the check determines the ability being used, and corresponds to one of the 3 abilities your Ally has.

> **Sidebar:** *Each Ally has 3 abilities: [Might] Might, [Agility] Agility, [Will] Will.*

These 3 abilities, and the level your Ally has in each, are shown at the bottom of your Hero board or Ally card. Some active ability checks may allow you to choose between 2 or more abilities. In this case, the ability symbols are separated by a '|'.

Before you roll the [Action Die], you must decide which optional effect(s) you are using (such as discarding an item to fulfill the requirements of a modifier, or using a once per scenario effect).

##### To calculate the number of successes achieved, add together: (p6)

- The number of successes you roll.
- Any modifiers shown in the check that you fulfill the requirements of.

The active ability check is successful if you achieve a total number of successes equal to or higher than the number specified by the check, shown in a red box.

> **Action dice**
>
> There are various symbols on an Action die:
>
> - [Action Die: 1 Success] 1 success
> - [Action Die: 2 Successes] 2 successes
> - [Action Die: Extra Roll] 1 success, and 1 extra roll

> **Sidebar:** [Action Die: Extra Roll] *Each extra roll allows you to roll an extra Action die and add its result to your total. If this roll gets you another extra roll then roll another Action die, and so on.*

> [Action Die: Stamina] While playing a scenario, a Hero may spend 1 or 2 stamina to get an equal number of successes.
> At all other times, this counts as 2 successes for a Hero, shown as [Action Die: Stamina] +2 [Success].
> For a Non-Hero Ally, this always counts as 0 successes.
> You may roll any extra rolls before deciding how much stamina you wish to spend.

##### Ability Check Modifiers (p6)

Modifiers may be shown in an ability check. If you fulfill the requirements of one or more modifiers, add those modifiers to the number of successes you roll. The types of requirement and when they are fulfilled are:

> **Sidebar — Example:** *Card: "Pick the Lock" (numbered "0"). Lock picking: +2 [Success]. [Agility] 6 Success » FLIP.*
>
> *Example: You get a modifier of +2 [Success] to this active agility check if you have Lock picking. You may get this from either having the Burglary Talent from the Streetwise profession, or from having Item card 001 "Thieves' Tools" as an active item.*
>
> *Illustration: below the example text, a "Burglary" profession card (listing "Lock picking, Disarm traps") and a "Lock picking" Talent card. Faint, apparently unrelated text is visible bled in above the "Pick the Lock" card art: "…smells much worse. It is too heavy to move."*

- Hero Name: If you are playing the Hero shown.
- Class: If your Hero is the Class shown.
- Profession: If your Hero has the Profession shown.
- Talent: If your Hero has a Talent of that name.
- Keyword: If the Keyword shown is in your Hero or Party Journal. Keywords are shown in quotation marks.
- Item card or token: If your Hero is carrying 1 or more of the item shown. During a scenario, if the Item card has a hand symbol on it, the item must be an active item for you to fulfil this requirement.
- [?icon: discard (card)] Item card or [?icon: discard (token)] token: If your Hero discards the Item card or token shown before they roll the [Action Die].
- Item card phrase: If an Item card you are carrying has a matching phrase on it.

If multiple requirements separated by commas are shown, you get the indicated modifier for each of the requirements you fulfill. Sometimes other modifiers may be shown. In this case the modifier will be explained in the check. Note that occasionally a modifier may be negative.

> **Sidebar — Example:** *Card: "Mine jade from the deposit" (faint, illegible tag above the title). Pickaxe: +2 [Success]. Shovel, Mining: +1 [Success]. [Might] 6. Success: Gain an Elemental Jade token from this card.*
>
> *Example: You get a modifier of +2 to this active might check if you have a pickaxe as an active item. You get a modifier of +1 if you have a shovel as an active item. You get a modifier of +1 if you have the Mining talent.*
>
> *Note that only Matajam (with his 4 hands) could get the bonus for both a pickaxe and a shovel on this check as they both require 2 hands.*

##### Active Ability Checks with a ? (p6)

Some active ability checks show a ? instead of specifying a number of successes. This is because either:

- It is a group ability check (see p. 8).
- The check has more than two different outcomes.
- The precise number of successes required is meant to be unknown.
