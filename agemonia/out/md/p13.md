#### Locations and Services (p9)

There are various locations in the city that your Heroes may visit, and various services at these locations that your Heroes may use. Your Heroes may visit any location and its services any number of times during each City step, and in any order unless instructed otherwise.

The locations and services available to your Heroes are detailed in the Campaign Book. Heroes may visit any location in the section of the Campaign Book (Level 1, Level2, etc) that is equal to their current level. Each time your Heroes gain a level, new locations and services become available, and some may disappear.

Services allow your Heroes to buy items, rent a room, obtain boons, find new scenarios, or possibly gain other benefits.

##### Buying Items (p9)

Some services offer items your Heroes may buy. Your Hero buys an item by spending Star equal to the cost shown, and taking the item from the Stock. If you have the opportunity to buy an item, all copies of the item in the Archive should be immediately added to the Stock.

> **Sidebar — Example:** *Your Hero visits The Agura Anvil to use the weapon shop service, and decides to purchase a sword. They spend 12 [Star] and take a Sword Item card from the Stock.*
>
> *Extract from a service listing:*
>
> - 5 [Star] » Item card 010 "Dagger"
> - 6 [Star] » Item card 015 "Bow"
> - 12 [Star] » Item card 009 "Sword"

##### Renting a Room (p9)

If your Hero finds a place to sleep, place your Hero miniature on the corresponding location on the city map.

If your Hero does not find a place to sleep, they lose 2 stamina at the end of the City step.

##### Boons (p9)

Some services offer unusual or unique boons.

> **Sidebar — Example:** *Your Hero may visit the Temple of the Fifth Eye in order to use the Heal Injury boon. This boon allows you to spend a City Activity point [City Activity Point] or 3 [Star] to discard 1 Injury card from your Hero.*
>
> *Extract from a service listing:*
>
> **Heal Injury:**
>
> [City Activity Point] or 3 [Star] » Discard 1 Injury card from your Hero.

##### Finding New Scenarios (p9)

Your Heroes may find a new scenario at a location. If they do, gain the Scenario card and add it to the Stock. See "Journey Step" on p. 11 for more information on Scenario cards.

##### Learning a Profession (p9)

Once your Hero is level 2, they may learn a profession. Each profession may only be learned by one Hero, and each Hero may only learn one profession.

To learn a profession, visit the relevant service and pay the cost shown. Take the Profession card. For more information, see "Professions" on p. 41.

##### Stashing Items and Stars (p9)

You may find places where items may be stashed. This is useful for hiding illegal items from the authorities or keeping your belongings safe from thieves and other unfortunate events. See "Illegal Items" on p. 10. You may not stash Quest items or Hero specific items.

Instead of stashing an item, you may stash up to 10 [Star].

Stashed items and Stars do not count as being carried by your Hero. You Hero may not use a stashed item or spend stashed Stars, but they may retrieve them at any time during the City step except when resolving a City Event card.

#### Selling Items (p9)

Your Hero may sell an item at any time during the City step. Place the sold item in the Stock and take an amount of Star equal to the sale value of the item.

The sale value of an Item card is shown on the coin on the card. If there is no coin shown, the item may not be sold.

> **Sidebar — Example:** *You decide to sell a Potion of Healing. You place the Potion of Healing Item card in the Stock and take 2 [Star].*
>
> *Card: an Item card, "Potion of Healing", with a sale-value coin reading 2 and an effect box reading `[Discard] [Heal] 5`.*

The sale value of an Item token is indicated by the size and shape of the token:

- Small circular Item tokens have a sale value of 1 [Star]
- Triangular Item tokens have a sale value of 3 [Star]
- Pentagonal Item tokens have a sale value of 5 [Star]
- Large circular Item tokens have a sale value of 10 [Star]
- Octagonal Item tokens have a sale value of 20 [Star]

*Illustrations: each bullet is flanked by a photograph of an Item token of that shape and by the matching value token, marked 3, 5, 10 and 20 respectively.*

> **Sidebar — Note:** Illegal items may not normally be sold. See "Illegal Items" on p. 10.

#### Quest Item cards (p9)

Quest Item cards have "Quest" on them. These cards may not be sold, discarded, or stashed.

#### Hero Specific Item cards (p9)

Hero Specific Item cards display the name of the Hero that may use them. These cards may not be given to any other Hero, sold, discarded, or stashed.
