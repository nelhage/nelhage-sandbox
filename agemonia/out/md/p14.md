#### Illegal Items (p10)

> **Sidebar — Illegal Item examples:**
>
> *Illustrations: eight Illegal Item tokens, each shown with the red-rimmed back that marks an item as illegal (including a green crystal cluster, a set of manacles, a hare, a dark red gem cluster, a coiled creature, and a red crystal shard), and the Illegal Item card "Thieves' Tools," with a sale-value coin reading 3 and an effect box reading "Lock picking." Other small stat icons on the card are rasterised too coarsely to read reliably.*

Illegal Item cards have a red background behind the name at the top of the card and have "illegal" shown underneath the name.

Illegal Item tokens have a red border on the side showing their effect.

Illegal items may not be sold unless you are instructed otherwise. Illegal items may be discarded or stashed.

Be warned, if your Heroes are caught carrying illegal items, there may be unfortunate consequences!

#### Resolve a City Event card (p10)

Once during each City step, after you have visited all the locations you wish to visit and used all the services you wish to use for now, reveal the top card of the City Event deck, read the description on the card, and follow the instructions.

Most City Event cards will require you to choose one of several options on the card. If an option has a requirement, you must fulfill that requirement to choose that option. Your chosen option will usually direct you to read an entry in the Story Book.

Heroes may exchange items while resolving the card, but may not sell items, visit any locations, use any services, or retrieve stashed items unless instructed otherwise.

> **Sidebar — Note:** Consumables can be used while resolving an Event card.

After resolving the card, remove it from play.

Heroes may then once again sell items, visit locations, use services, and retrieve stashed items as well as spend their City Activity points until the end of the City step.

#### [City Activity Point] City Activity points (p10)

During each City step, each Hero may normally spend up to 2 City Activity points [City Activity Point]. Various effects may change the number of points you actually get to spend. Your Hero book, certain locations in the Campaign Book, and various cards show how these points may be spent.

> **Sidebar — Example:** *Your Hero visits Anglers' Pier and decides to work on the docks. They spend 1 [City Activity Point] and gain 2 [Star]. They have 1 [City Activity Point] remaining. They may spend it to work on the docks again, or on some other activity.*
>
> *Card: an Activity card, showing a City Activity Point token icon beside the heading "Activity: Work on the Do[cks]" (printed cut off at the edge of the page), reading "You find work carrying crates and ch[...]" (also cut off), with an effect row reading `[City Activity Point] » Gain 2 [Star]`.*

City Activity points may not be spent until after the City Event card has been revealed and resolved. Once the City Event card has been resolved, take a number of Activity tokens equal to the number of City Activity points you have (2 unless otherwise instructed). Remember, you do not reveal a City Event card and may not spend any City Activity points in the City step following an arena scenario..

When you spend a City Activity point, discard an Activity token.

Unspent Activity tokens are discarded at the end of the City step.

#### End of the City Step (p10)

Once a City Event card has been resolved (or at any time in the City step following an Arena scenario), you may agree to end the City step.

Remember, if your Hero miniature is in the Disturbed Sleep location at the end of the City step, your Hero loses 2 stamina
