### 2. Journey Step (p11)

Choose a Scenario card in the Stock. Some Scenario cards have requirements; you may only choose a Scenario card if you have fulfilled its requirements. You may not choose an arena Scenario card if you played an arena scenario in the previous Scenario step.

#### Scenario cards (p11)

> **Sidebar:** *Scenario card back:*
>
> *Illustration: the reverse side of a Scenario card, decorated with a green-and-gold ornamental border framing a circular emblem of mountains above a winding road.*

Each Scenario card shows the number, name, requirements (if any), duration, location illustration, introduction, and numbered journey stages for a particular scenario.

If the card shows an experience symbol [Experience], completing the scenario via a Scenario Success or Fail Forward outcome will gain you an experience point (see Experience Points and Gaining a Level on p. 27). Completing a scenario via a Fail outcome does not gain you an experience point and you do not record the Fail on your Party sheet. You may replay that scenario.

If the Scenario card shows the replay symbol [Replay], the Scenario card is not removed from the game after playing the scenario unless you are instructed otherwise.

If the Scenario card shows the arena symbol [Arena], the scenario is an arena scenario. Arena scenarios may be played multiple times, although you may not play two arena scenarios consecutively.

##### Numbered journey stages (p11)

Read the introduction on the Scenario card carefully and follow the numbered journey stages shown on the card in order. Each journey stage directs you to do one of the following:

- Read an entry in the Story Book.
- Visit a special location.
- Resolve one or more World Event cards.
- Play the corresponding scenario.

##### Split Journey Stages (p11)

> **Sidebar — Example:** *This journey is split into **A** and **B** stages. You may choose either, however each Hero must spend 2 [Star] to choose stage **1A**. If you cannot, or do not wish to spend this, you must instead choose stage **1B**.*
>
> *If you chose stage **1A**, you must choose stage **2A** and visit special location [Story Point C] Maddark Seat.*
>
> *If you chose stage **1B**, you must choose stage **2B**, and visit special location [Story Point A] Skyfford Ruins.*
>
> *Illustration: a Scenario card's Journey box, captioned above the banner "Journey" with the faint bled-through text "...the key to the Vault of Time.":*
> *1A: Each Hero spends 2 [Star]: Take the river south » [Story Book] 987*
> *2A: [Story Point C] Maddark Seat » [Story Book] 657*
> *1B: Take the long road southwest » [Story Book] 789*
> *2B: [Story Point A] Skyfford Ruins » [Story Book] 223*
> *3. Resolve two World Event cards.*
> *4. Play the scenario [?icon: two purple faceted circular tokens] » [Scenario Book] p. 36*

Some numbered stages are split into an A and a B stage. In this case, choose one of the two lettered journey stages to follow. Some of these stages have a requirement, such as spending an amount of Star. You may only choose a lettered stage if you fulfill this requirement.

If the numbered stage after a split journey stage is also split, you must choose the stage with the same letter as you chose previously.

##### Visiting Special Locations (p11)

To visit a special location, read the entry number in the Story Book shown in the stage. Special locations have ongoing plots and storylines which advance each time you visit the special location.

##### Resolving World Event cards (p11)

> **Sidebar — Note:** *Consumables can be used while resolving an Event card.*

Some journey stages will instruct you to resolve a World Event card. Reveal the top card of the World Event deck, read the description on the card, and follow the instructions.

Particularly long journeys may instruct you resolve more than one World Event card. If you are instructed to resolve more than one World Event card, resolve them one at a time.

Most World Event cards will require you to choose one of several options on the card. If an option has a requirement, you must fulfill that requirement to choose that option. Your chosen option will usually direct you to read an entry in the Story Book.

After resolving the card, remove it from play.
