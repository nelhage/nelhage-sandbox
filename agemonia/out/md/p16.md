## Scenario Rules (p12)

### Scenario Setup (p12)

> *Illustration: scenario components laid out for setup — a double-sized Scenario map board and the Scenario book open to a page (1, 10), the Story deck (2), Initiative deck (3), Fate cards (4), Injury cards (5), Condition cards (6), Condition tokens (7), Enemy boards (8), Action/Reaction/Counter dice (9), the Scenario book's additional setup page (11), and a Hero miniature on the map (12) — each labelled with a numbered red-diamond callout matching the steps below.*

#### Main (p12)

1. Place the Scenario book in the middle of the play area and open it to the page shown on the Scenario card.
2. Place the Story deck for the chosen scenario face down to the right of the Scenario book. Do not shuffle this deck or read any of the Story cards.

> **Sidebar — Note:** *If you are playing an arena scenario, there is no Story deck.*

3. Shuffle the Initiative deck and place it face down to the right of the Scenario book.
   **Note:** The cards you use in your Initiative deck depend on the difficulty level you decide to play this scenario at, see p. 60.
4. Shuffle the Fate cards and place them face down to the right of the Scenario book to create the Fate deck.
5. Shuffle the Injury cards and place them face down below the Scenario book to create the Injury deck.
6. Place the Condition cards in a face up pile below the Scenario book. Do not shuffle these cards.
7. Place the Condition tokens in a pile next to the Condition cards.
8. Place the Enemy boards in a stack below the Scenario book.
9. Place the Action dice, Reaction dice, and Counter dice within easy reach of all players.

#### Scenario Specific (p12)

10. Choose one player to be the narrator. That player reads aloud the introduction in the Scenario book for your chosen scenario. If the Scenario book does not show a scenario map, the scenario uses one or more of the separate double-sized maps.
11. Follow the additional setup shown in the Scenario book for your chosen scenario. If there is a conflict between the default setup rules and the additional setup in a scenario, the additional setup in the scenario takes precedence.
