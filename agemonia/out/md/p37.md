### Hero Actions (p33)

Many of the Hero actions use common effects. These are described below, followed by a detailed description of each Hero action.

##### [Attack] Attack Actions (p33)

When you perform an attack action [Attack] you must make any choices about which Enemies or areas you are targeting before rolling any [Action Die].

When you roll [Action Die: Stamina] on one or more [Action Die] during an attack that targets several Enemies, any stamina you spend will increase the damage suffered by all Enemies targeted by the attack.

##### Marking Allies, Enemies, Areas, and Weapons (p33)

Some Hero actions use one or more tokens to mark Allies, Enemies, areas of the Scenario map, or Item cards.

To mark an Ally, place the token on that Ally's Hero board or Ally card.

To mark an Enemy, place the token next to that Enemy's Health Counter die on its Enemy board.

To mark an area of the Scenario map, place the token in that area.

To mark an Item card, place the token on that Item card.

If you perform the action again and the token(s) are still placed, take them back and place them again.

You may want to place the token in a stand to make it more visible.

##### Cleave (p33)

You may share the damage caused by an attack with cleave in any way you like among several Enemies within range. You do not need to decide which Enemy you are attacking before you roll the attack.

##### Passive Actions (p33)

All level 3 Hero actions are passive actions. Passive actions require you to lock 1 or more stamina (see Locking Stamina on p. 29).

If a passive action ends:

- Place any of its remaining locked Stamina chips above your Hero board, stamina side up.
- If the passive action uses one or more tokens, take back those tokens.

If you perform a passive action while the same passive action is already active, the previous passive action ends.

You may voluntarily end a passive action at any time.

##### [Area] Area attacks (p33)

Area attacks affect every Enemy in one or more areas.

If a range is also shown, the area of the attack is centered on an area of your choice within that range. If no range is shown, the area of the attack is centered on the area containing the Ally performing the attack.

The number in the hex symbol shows at what range areas are affected.

All Enemies in the area within the given range are affected by the area attack. An area must be within line of sight of the targeted area to be affected. Areas of a different height may be affected by the attack as long as they are in line of sight.

Obstructed aim penalty does not apply to any damage caused by an area attack.

##### [?icon: two filled hexagons stacked vertically — adjacent areas] Adjacent Area Attacks (p33)

Target all Enemies in 2 adjacent areas.

##### [Range] 1-3 [?icon: three filled hexagons in a diagonal line — straight line] Straight Line Attacks (p33)

Target up to one Enemy in each of the 3 areas in a straight line of sight from your area. Obstructed aim penalty does not apply to any damage caused by a straight line attack.

Any effect that increases or decreases the maximum range of a magic attack also increases or decreases the maximum range and therefore the number of areas a straight line attack affects. For example, an effect that increases the maximum range of your magic attacks by 1 allows a range 3 straight line attack to target up to one Enemy in each of the 4 areas in a straight line of sight from your area.

#### Focus Action (p33)

Selecting the focus action allows you to roll 1 extra die during every active and reactive ability check you perform this round, even before your turn. If you use the focus action to roll an extra die, you may no longer change your action to the rethink action on your turn.

#### Extra Maneuver Action (p33)

The extra maneuver action allows you to perform another maneuver during your turn.
