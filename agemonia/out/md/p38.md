### Drenosh (p34)

#### Drenosh · Level 1 (p34)

##### Judicious Shot (p34)

Make a melee or ranged attack using 2 [Action Die].

*Sidebar card: [Ally Slow] · [Melee Attack] | [Ranged Attack] + 2 [Action Die].*

##### Fiery Strike (p34)

Spend 2 stamina. Make a melee or ranged attack using 3 [Action Die].

*Sidebar card: [Ally Medium] · 2 [Stamina] · [Melee Attack] | [Ranged Attack] + 3 [Action Die].*

##### Disengage (p34)

Spend 1 stamina. Make a melee attack using 2 [Action Die]. You get Evade 1 this turn.

*Sidebar card: [Ally Fast] · 1 [Stamina] · [Melee Attack] + 2 [Action Die], Evade 1.*

#### Drenosh · Level 2 (p34)

##### Flaming Fury (p34)

Spend 4 stamina. Make a melee attack using 2 [Action Die], and then another using 1 [Action Die].

*Sidebar card: [Ally Medium] · 4 [Stamina] · [Melee Attack] + 2 [Action Die] » + 1 [Action Die].*

##### Charge (Marauder) (p34)

Spend 3 stamina. Make a melee attack using 2 [Action Die]. This attack gets +1 success for each movement point you have spent on moving this turn.

*Sidebar card: [Ally Fast] · 3 [Stamina] · [Melee Attack] + 2 [Action Die], +1 [Success] × [Movement Point].*

##### Fire Blast (Pyroblade) (p34)

Spend 2 stamina. Make a range 4 magic attack using 3 [Action Die].

*Sidebar card: [Ally Slow] · 2 [Stamina] · [Magic Attack] [Range] 4 + 3 [Action Die].*

#### Drenosh · Level 3 (p34)

##### Fiery Weapon (p34)

A passive action. When you lock 1 stamina on this action, choose your Hero or another Hero in the same area as your Hero. Mark a melee weapon that Hero is carrying with the Fiery Weapon token. Each time the marked weapon is used to make a melee attack, add 1 success to that attack. The marked weapon counts as a Light source.

*Sidebar card: [Ally Medium] · [Lock Stamina] 1 · [Range] 0, [Melee Attack] Item: Fiery Weapon token; +1 [Success], Light.*

##### Sneak (Marauder) (p34)

A passive action. When you lock 2 stamina on this action, gain the Invisible condition. If you lose the Invisible condition or perform a recover maneuver, this passive action ends. If this passive action ends, you lose the Invisible condition.

*Sidebar card: [Ally Medium] · [Lock Stamina] 2 · [Invisible], [Ends]: [?icon: lose Invisible condition] | [?icon: perform a recover maneuver].*

##### Fire Trap (Pyroblade) (p34)

A passive action. When you lock 1 stamina on this action, mark the area you are in with the Fire Trap token. If an Enemy moves into the marked area, that Enemy suffers 3 damage and this passive action ends.

*Sidebar card: [Ally Fast] · [Lock Stamina] 1 · [Range] 0, Fire Trap token: 3 [Damage]. [Ends]: Enemy enters Fire Trap token area.*

#### Drenosh · Level 4 (p34)

##### Curse of Blood (p34)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Mark an Enemy in line of sight within range 4 with the Curse of Blood token. The marked Enemy permanently gains the Weakened condition while it is marked. If the marked Enemy is defeated, take back the Curse of Blood token.

*Sidebar card: [Ally Fast] · [Inspiration Gem] · [?icon: range/target marker] Curse of Blood token: [Weakened]. [Ends]: Enemy Defeated.*

##### Bladefury (Marauder) (p34)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Roll a melee attack using 2 [Action Die]. This attack targets all Enemies in line of sight in area 1.

*Sidebar card: [Ally Fast] · [Inspiration Gem] · [Melee Attack] [Area] 1 + 2 [Action Die].*

##### Hellfire (Pyroblade) (p34)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Choose a number equal to or lower than the amount of Stamina chips you have that are stamina side up (unspent, spent, or locked). Suffer damage equal to the chosen number. Make an area 1 magic attack using 0 [Action Die] + a number of successes equal to the chosen number.

*Sidebar card: [Ally Slow] · [Inspiration Gem] · [Magic Attack] [Area] 1, 0 [Action Die] / [?icon: variable damage-to-successes notation].*

#### Drenosh · Level 5 (p34)

##### Intercept (p34)

Spend 3 stamina. Make a melee attack using 3 [Action Die]. Also, see "Special Initiatives" on p. 29.

*Sidebar card: [First] · 3 [Stamina] · [Melee Attack] + 3 [Action Die].*

##### Chaos Strike (Marauder) (p34)

Spend 3 stamina. Make a melee or ranged attack using 3 [Action Die]. This attack gets +2 successes if the target of the attack is at full health.

*Sidebar card: [Ally Medium] · 3 [Stamina] · [Melee Attack] + 3 [Action Die]; Enemy at full health: +2 [Success].*

##### Wall of Fire (Pyroblade) (p34)

Spend 4 stamina. Mark 2 adjacent areas in line of sight within range 3 with a Wall of Fire token. Each time an Enemy moves into either of the marked areas, they suffer 2 damage.

*Sidebar card: [Ally Slow] · 4 [Stamina] · [Range] 3, Wall of Fire token ×2: 2 [Damage].*
