### Jonai (p35)

*Sidebar: a column of Jonai Hero Action cards, one per action listed at right, in the same top-to-bottom order, each showing the action's name and its cost and effect in icon notation matching the prose at right (e.g. Radiant Shot's card reads [Ranged Attack] + 2 [Action Die], Aox: +1 [Success]). Each card also carries a small coloured roundel at its left edge showing the action's initiative — [Ally Fast] (red disc, triangle), [Ally Medium] (blue disc, diamond), [Ally Slow] (green disc, circle) — or on Reactive Attack the special [Ally] initiative token (rainbow disc reading "Ally"): True Strike [Ally Slow], Defender of Mion [Ally Medium], Channel Energy [Ally Fast], Reactive Attack [Ally], Blades of Wrath [Ally Slow], Radiant Shot [Ally Medium], Spell Shield [Ally Medium], War Dancer [Ally Fast], Sentinel's Mark [Ally Medium], Alteration [Ally Fast], Split in Two [Ally Medium], Summon Arrow [Ally Slow], Phasing Strike [Ally Medium], Bladestorm [Ally Fast], Arrowstorm [Ally Fast].*

#### Jonai · Level 1 (p35)

##### True Strike (p35)

Make a melee or ranged attack using 1 [Action Die]. This attack gets +2 successes.

##### Defender of Mion (p35)

Spend 1 stamina. Make a melee attack using 2 [Action Die]. Gain 1 shield during each reactive ability check against an attack until the end of the round.

##### Channel Energy (p35)

Mark another Ally in line of sight within range 3 with the Channel Energy token. The next time the marked Ally rolls 1 or more [Action Die] or [Reaction Die], they roll 2 more dice and you take back the Channel Energy token.

*Illustration: the Channel Energy token, a round Jonai-coloured marker.*

#### Jonai · Level 2 (p35)

##### Reactive Attack (p35)

Spend 1 stamina. Make a melee or ranged attack using 2 [Action Die]. Also, see "Special Initiatives" on p. 29.

##### Blades of Wrath (Blademaster) (p35)

Spend 3 stamina. Make a melee attack using 1 [Action Die], and then another using 1 [Action Die].

##### Radiant Shot (Aox Hunter) (p35)

Make a ranged attack using 2 [Action Die]. If you are attacking an Aox Enemy, this attack gets +1 success.

#### Jonai · Level 3 (p35)

##### Spell Shield (p35)

A passive action. When you lock 1 stamina on this action, mark your Hero or another Ally in line of sight within range 2 with the Spell Shield token. The marked Ally has Riposte 2 against all magic attacks.

*Illustration: the Spell Shield token, a round Jonai-coloured marker.*

##### War Dancer (Blademaster) (p35)

A passive action. If there is 1 stamina locked on this action, your Hero recovers an amount of spent stamina equal to any Overkill damage that an attack you make does.

##### Sentinel's Mark (Aox Hunter) (p35)

A passive action. When you lock 1 stamina on this action, mark an Enemy in line of sight within range 3 with the Sentinel's Mark token. Each time your Hero attacks the marked Enemy, roll an [Action Die] and add the number of successes you roll to the damage you do to that Enemy.

*Illustration: the Sentinel's Mark token, a round Jonai-coloured marker.*

#### Jonai · Level 4 (p35)

##### Alteration (p35)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Choose your Hero or another Hero in line of sight within range 3. The chosen Hero may flip all of their Stamina chips that are wound side up to their stamina side. The Stamina chips are still spent.

##### Split in Two (Blademaster) (p35)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Roll a melee attack using 3 [Action Die]. Double the attacks total successes before applying the damage.

##### Summon Arrow (Aox Hunter) (p35)

Spend an Inspiration gem. If Item card 064 "Heartseeker Arrow" is in the Stock, gain it. If you still have this Item card at the end of the scenario, discard it.

#### Jonai · Level 5 (p35)

##### Phasing Strike (p35)

Spend 2 stamina. Make a melee attack using 3 [Action Die]. You may Move 1, either before or after this attack.

##### Bladestorm (Blademaster) (p35)

Spend 2 stamina. Roll a melee attack using 3 [Action Die]. This attack has range 1 and Cleave.

##### Arrowstorm (Aox Hunter) (p35)

Spend 4 stamina. Make a ranged attack using 1 [Action Die], then another using 1 [Action Die], and then another using 1 [Action Die].
