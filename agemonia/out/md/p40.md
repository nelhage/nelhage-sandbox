### Lunara (p36)

*Sidebar: a column of Lunara Hero Action cards, one per action listed at left, in the same top-to-bottom order, each showing the action's name and its cost and effect in icon notation matching the prose at left (e.g. Shield Bash's card reads Shield: 2 [Action Die], Push·1). Each card also carries a small coloured roundel at its left edge showing the action's initiative — [Ally Fast] (red disc, triangle), [Ally Medium] (blue disc, diamond), [Ally Slow] (green disc, circle): Stalwart Smite [Ally Medium], Flash Heal [Ally Fast], Gallant Strike [Ally Slow], Shield Bash [Ally Medium], Debilitate [Ally Fast], Blade of Justice [Ally Slow], Guardian of Light [Ally Fast], Aura of Courage [Ally Fast], Consecrate [Ally Slow], Revival [Ally Slow], Armor of Faith [Ally Fast], Light of Aiun [Ally Fast], Weapon of Fate [Ally Fast], Righteous Fury [Ally Medium], Divine Shield [Ally Medium].*

#### Lunara · Level 1 (p36)

##### Stalwart Smite (p36)

Make a melee or ranged attack using 2 [Action Die].

##### Flash Heal (p36)

Choose your Hero or another Ally in line of sight within range 2. Roll 3 [Action Die]. The chosen Ally heals an amount of their damage equal to the number of successes rolled.

##### Gallant Strike (p36)

Spend 2 stamina. Make a melee or ranged attack using 3 [Action Die].

#### Lunara · Level 2 (p36)

##### Shield Bash (p36)

Requires a shield as an active item. Make a melee attack using 2 [Action Die]. No other weapon modifiers may be used during this attack. This attack has Push·1.

##### Debilitate (Knight) (p36)

Spend 2 stamina. Make a melee or ranged attack using 2 [Action Die]. An Enemy that takes damage from this attack gains the Weakened condition.

##### Blade of Justice (Paladin) (p36)

Spend 4 stamina. Make a melee attack using a number of [Action Die] equal to 2 plus your Will.

#### Lunara · Level 3 (p36)

##### Guardian of Light (p36)

A passive action. If there is 1 stamina locked on this action, it counts as a light source and any healing effect (including the recover maneuver) performed by Lunara or on Lunara heals an extra 1 damage.

##### Aura of Courage (Knight) (p36)

A passive action. If there is 2 stamina locked on this action, whenever your Hero or another Ally in line of sight within area 2 makes a melee or ranged attack, add 1 success to that attack.

##### Consecrate (Paladin) (p36)

A passive action. When you lock 2 stamina on this action, place the Consecrate token in the same area as your Hero. Any Ally in the same area as the Consecrate token and who is not hindered may, once on their turn, heal 2 of their damage.

*Illustration: the Consecrate token, a round Lunara-coloured marker.*

#### Lunara · Level 4 (p36)

##### Revival (p36)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Choose your Hero or another Ally in the same area as your Hero, and one of the following effects:

- Heal 5 damage to the chosen Ally.
- Remove wounded status from the chosen Ally.

##### Armor of Faith (Knight) (p36)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Mark your Hero or another Ally in line of sight within range 5 with the Armor of Faith token. The marked Ally gains Mirror against the next melee attack that targets them. After this attack, take back the Armor of Faith token.

*Illustration: the Armor of Faith token, a round Lunara-coloured marker.*

##### Light of Aiun (Paladin) (p36)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Push·2 all Enemies in line of sight within range 1. All Allies in line of sight within range 1 heal 3 of their damage and gain the Blessed condition.

#### Lunara · Level 5 (p36)

##### Weapon of Fate (p36)

Spend 3 stamina. Make a melee attack using 3 [Action Die]. Heal an amount of damage to your Hero equal to any Overkill damage this attack does.

##### Righteous Fury (Knight) (p36)

Spend 3 stamina. Make a melee attack using 2 [Action Die]. Additionally, all Enemies in line of sight within range 1 suffer 2 damage.

##### Divine Shield (Paladin) (p36)

Spend 2 stamina. Mark your Hero or another Ally in line of sight within range 3 with the Divine Shield token. The next time the marked Ally rolls a Reaction check against an attack, they get 2 extra successes and gain the Blessed condition. After this attack, take back the Divine Shield token.

*Illustration: the Divine Shield token, a round Lunara-coloured marker.*
