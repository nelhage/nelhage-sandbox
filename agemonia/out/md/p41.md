### Matajam (p37)

*Sidebar: a column of Matajam Hero Action cards, one per action listed at right, in the same top-to-bottom order, each showing the action's name and its cost and effect in icon notation matching the prose at right (e.g. Twin Shot's card reads [Ranged Attack] + 2 [Action Die] » +1 [Action Die]). Each card also carries a small coloured roundel at its left edge showing the action's initiative — [Ally Fast] (red disc, triangle), [Ally Medium] (blue disc, diamond), [Ally Slow] (green disc, circle): Swift Strike [Ally Fast], Aimed Shot [Ally Medium], Dancing Blade [Ally Fast], Raging Nalam [Ally Fast], Twin Shot [Ally Slow], Bane [Ally Medium], Enchanted Nalam [Ally Medium], Stun Trap [Ally Fast], Insect Swarm [Ally Slow], Spirit Transfer [Ally Slow], Multi-Shot [Ally Medium], Jinx [Ally Fast], Hypnotic Gaze [Ally Fast], Slashing Blades [Ally Medium], Elemental Surge [Ally Slow].*

#### Matajam · Level 1 (p37)

##### Swift Strike (p37)

Make a melee or ranged attack using 2 [Action Die].

##### Aimed Shot (p37)

Spend 1 stamina. Make a ranged attack using 3 [Action Die].

##### Dancing Blade (p37)

Spend 1 stamina. Make a melee attack using 2 [Action Die]. You may move 1 either before or after this attack.

#### Matajam · Level 2 (p37)

##### Raging Nalam (p37)

Spend 2 stamina. Your Nalam makes a melee attack using 2 [Action Die]. An Enemy that takes damage from this attack gains the Slowed condition.

##### Twin Shot (Ranger) (p37)

Spend 3 stamina. Make a ranged attack using 2 [Action Die], and then another using 1 [Action Die].

##### Bane (Shaman) (p37)

Spend 1 stamina. Roll a magic attack using 2 [Action Die]. This attack targets all Enemies in one area in line of sight within range 3.

#### Matajam · Level 3 (p37)

##### Enchanted Nalam (p37)

A passive action. If there is 1 stamina locked on this action, your Nalam may spend 1 extra movement point each time it moves and its Provoke increases by 1.

*Illustration: the Enchanted Nalam token, a round Matajam-coloured marker.*

##### Stun Trap (Ranger) (p37)

A passive action. When you lock 3 stamina on this action, mark the area your Hero is in with the Stun Trap token. If an Enemy moves into the marked area, that Enemy gains the Stunned condition and this passive action ends.

*Illustration: the Stun Trap token, a round Matajam-coloured marker.*

##### Insect Swarm (Shaman) (p37)

A passive action. When you lock 2 stamina on this action, summon the Insect Swarm token in line of sight within range 1.

*Illustration: the Insect Swarm token, a round Matajam-coloured marker.*

Once on each of your subsequent turns, the Swarm may move 1 and attack. This attack does 2 damage and targets all Enemies in the same area as the Insect Swarm. Each Enemy that takes damage from this attack gains the Slowed condition.

#### Matajam · Level 4 (p37)

##### Spirit Transfer (p37)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Place your Hero in the same area as your Nalam.

##### Multi-Shot (Ranger) (p37)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Make a ranged attack using 1 [Action Die], then another using 1 [Action Die], and then another using 1 [Action Die].

##### Jinx (Shaman) (p37)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Mark a non-Elite Enemy in line of sight within range 4 with the Jinx token, Jinx symbol side up. The marked Enemy gains the Dominated condition for as long as it is marked.

*Illustration: the Jinx token, a round Matajam-coloured marker.*

At the start of your next turn, flip the Jinx token to its Matajam symbol side. At the start of your next turn after that, this effect ends. This effect also ends if the marked Enemy is defeated.

When this effect ends, take back the Jinx token.

#### Matajam · Level 5 (p37)

##### Hypnotic Gaze (p37)

Spend 3 stamina. Choose a non-Elite Enemy in the same area as your Nalam. The chosen Enemy gains the Stunned condition.

##### Slashing Blades (Ranger) (p37)

Spend 3 stamina. Make a melee attack using 3 [Action Die]. Your Hero recovers an amount of spent stamina equal to any Overkill damage this attack does.

##### Elemental Surge (Shaman) (p37)

Spend 3 stamina. Roll a magic attack using 2 [Action Die]. This attack targets one Enemy in each of the first 3 areas in a straight line of sight from your area. Each Enemy that takes damage from this attack gains the Bleeding condition.
