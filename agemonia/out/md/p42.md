> **Sidebar — Colossal Crush card:** [Ally Slow] 1 [Stamina] [Melee Attack] + 3 [Action Die]

##### Boulder (p38)

Spend 1 stamina. Make a range 3 magic attack using 2 [Action Die].

> **Sidebar — Boulder card:** [Ally Slow] 1 [Stamina] [Magic Attack] [Range] 3 2 [Action Die]

#### Torrax · Level 2 (p38)

##### Telekinesis (p38)

Move an item in line of sight within range 3 to another area in line of sight within range 3. If the item is being carried by a Hero, they must agree to the item being moved. If there is a Hero in the area the item is moved to, you may give the item to that Hero.

> **Sidebar — Telekinesis card:** [Ally Fast] [Range] 3 Item: Move [Range] 3

##### Grapple (Guardian) (p38)

Spend 1 stamina. Pull·2 an Enemy within range 2 and make a melee attack using 2 [Action Die] against them.

> **Sidebar — Grapple card:** [Ally Medium] 1 [Stamina] [Range] 2 Pull·2 [Melee Attack] + 2 [Action Die]

##### Earth Bolt (Geomancer) (p38)

Spend 3 stamina. Roll a magic attack using 2 [Action Die]. This attack targets up to one Enemy in each of the first 3 areas in a straight line of sight from your area. Each Enemy that takes damage from this attack gains the Slowed condition.

> **Sidebar — Earth Bolt card:** [Ally Slow] 3 [Stamina] [Magic Attack] [Range] 1-3 [?icon: three filled hexagons in a diagonal line — straight line] 2 [Action Die] + [Slowed]

#### Torrax · Level 3 (p38)

##### Reconstruction (p38)

A passive action. If there are 2 stamina locked on this action, once on each of your turns, you may heal 1 of your damage.

> **Sidebar — Reconstruction card:** [Ally Medium] [Lock Stamina] 2 [Heal] 1 /turn

##### Battle Stance (Guardian) (p38)

A passive action. If there is 1 stamina locked on this action, you have Riposte 2 against all melee attacks within range 1.

> **Sidebar — Battle Stance card:** [Ally Fast] [Lock Stamina] 1 [Range] 1 Riposte·2 ([Melee Attack])

##### Energy Absorption (Geomancer) (p38)

A passive action. When you lock 1 stamina on this action, mark your Hero or another Ally in line of sight within range 2 with the Energy Absorption token. The marked Ally may recover 2 spent stamina after each time they are attacked.

*Illustration: the Energy Absorption token.*

> **Sidebar — Energy Absorption card:** [Ally Medium] [Lock Stamina] 1 [Range] 2 (the Energy Absorption token) After Enemy [Restore Stamina] 2

#### Torrax · Level 4 (p38)

##### Stone Form (p38)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Place the Stone Form token Stone Form symbol side up in the same area as your Hero. You now take up 2 capacity in that area. If that area is now above its maximum capacity, Push·1 one normal sized figure in that area if possible.

*Illustration: the Stone Form token.*

If your Hero is in an area with a maximum capacity of 1 or 2, no Ally or Enemy may move into that area.

Discard any conditions on your Hero. You do not take a turn and may not do anything during any other Ally or Enemy’s turn. You may not be affected by any attack, condition, or other effect.

At the start of the next round, flip the Stone Form token to its Torrax symbol side.

At the start of the next round after that, choose an action as normal. At the start of that turn, this effect ends. You may end this effect early, at the start of any Ally or Enemy turn (including your own).

When this effect ends, take back the Stone Form token and recover 5 stamina.

> **Sidebar — Stone Form card:** [Ally Fast] [Inspiration Gem] (the Stone Form token) Invulnerable, no Turn, Large [Ends]: 2 Rounds [Restore Stamina] 5

##### Pulverizing Blow (Guardian) (p38)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Make a melee attack using 5 [Action Die]. Each [Action Die: Extra Roll] rolled may be used as 2 successes instead of its normal effect.

> **Sidebar — Pulverizing Blow card:** [Ally Medium] [Inspiration Gem] [Melee Attack] + 5 [Action Die] [Action Die: Extra Roll] +2 [Success]

##### Earthquake (Geomancer) (p38)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Make an area 1 magic attack using 2 [Action Die]. Each Enemy that takes damage from this attack gains the Stunned condition.

> **Sidebar — Earthquake card:** [Ally Slow] [Inspiration Gem] [Magic Attack] [Area] 1 2 [Action Die] + [Stunned]
