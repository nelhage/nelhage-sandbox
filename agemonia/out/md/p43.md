#### Venia · Level 2 (p39)

##### Bounding Charge (p39)

Spend 2 stamina. Make a melee attack using 2 [Action Die]. You may Jump 2, either before or after this attack.

> **Sidebar — Bounding Charge card:** [Ally Fast] 2 [Stamina] [Melee Attack] + 2 [Action Die] Jump·2

##### Backstab (Assassin) (p39)

Spend 2 stamina. Make a melee attack using 4 [Action Die]. You may only target an Enemy with this attack if they are in an area that contains another Ally or a Pet figure.

> **Sidebar — Backstab card:** [Ally Fast] 2 [Stamina] With other Ally/Pet: [Melee Attack] + 4 [Action Die]

##### Shadow Tentacles (Witch) (p39)

Spend 3 stamina. Make an area 1 magic attack using 2 [Action Die].

> **Sidebar — Shadow Tentacles card:** [Ally Medium] 3 [Stamina] [Magic Attack] [Area] 1 2 [Action Die]

#### Venia · Level 3 (p39)

##### Shadow Form (p39)

A passive action. When you lock 1 stamina on this action, gain the Invisible condition. If you lose the Invisible condition, this passive action ends. If this passive action ends, you lose the Invisible condition.

> **Sidebar — Shadow Form card:** [Ally Fast] [Lock Stamina] 1 [Invisible]

##### Lacerate (Assassin) (p39)

A passive action. If there is 1 stamina locked on this action, each Enemy that takes damage from one of your melee attacks gains the Bleeding condition.

> **Sidebar — Lacerate card:** [Ally Fast] [Lock Stamina] 1 [Melee Attack] + [Bleeding]

##### Curse of Aox (Witch) (p39)

A passive action. When you lock 2 stamina on this action, mark an Enemy in line of sight within range 3 with the Curse of Aox token. Once on each of your turns, you may Drain 2 the marked Enemy. If the marked Enemy is defeated, this passive action ends.

*Illustration: the Curse of Aox token.*

> **Sidebar — Curse of Aox card:** [Ally Medium] [Lock Stamina] 2 [Range] 3 (the Curse of Aox token): Drain·2

##### Assassinate (Assassin) (p39)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Choose a non-Elite Enemy in the same area as your Hero and another Ally or a Pet. The chosen Enemy is defeated.

> **Sidebar — Assassinate card:** [Ally Fast] [Inspiration Gem] [Range] 0 With other Ally/Pet: Non-Elite: Defeat

##### Witch's Flight (Witch) (p39)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Jump 6 and gain Evade 1 this turn.

> **Sidebar — Witch's Flight card:** [Ally Slow] [Inspiration Gem] Jump·6, Evade·1

#### Venia · Level 5 (p39)

##### Spider Web (p39)

Spend 2 stamina. Mark 2 adjacent areas in line of sight within range 4 with a Spider Web token. Each Enemy that moves into either of these 2 areas gains the Slowed condition.

*Illustration: the Spider Web token.*

> **Sidebar — Spider Web card:** [Ally Slow] 2 [Stamina] [Range] 4 [?icon: two filled hexagons stacked vertically — adjacent areas] (the Spider Web token) [Slowed]

##### Hamstring (Assassin) (p39)

Spend 3 stamina. Make a melee attack using 2 [Action Die]. An Enemy that takes damage from this attack gains the Weakened condition. You may Move 2, either before or after this attack.

> **Sidebar — Hamstring card:** [Ally Medium] 3 [Stamina] [Melee Attack] + 2 [Action Die] + [Weakened] » [Movement Point] 2

##### Life Transference (Witch) (p39)

Your Hero suffers 2 damage. Another Ally in line of sight within range 3 recovers spent stamina and heals damage, in any combination, up to a total of 5.

> **Sidebar — Life Transference card:** [Ally Fast] 2 [Damage] [Range] 3 [Restore Stamina] | [Heal] 5 (Any combination)
