### Zuva'Sai (p40)

#### Zuva'Sai · Level 1 (p40)

##### Runic Trajection (p40)

Make a range 3 magic attack using 2 [Action Die].

> **Sidebar — Runic Trajection card:** [Ally Slow] [Magic Attack] [Range] 3 2 [Action Die]

##### Runic Blast (p40)

Spend 1 stamina. Make a range 2 magic attack using 3 [Action Die].

> **Sidebar — Runic Blast card:** [Ally Medium] 1 [Stamina] [Magic Attack] [Range] 2 3 [Action Die]

##### Empower (p40)

Choose your Hero or another Hero within range 3. Roll 3 [Action Die]. The chosen Hero recovers an amount of spent stamina equal to the number of successes rolled.

> **Sidebar — Empower card:** [Ally Fast] [Range] 3 [Restore Stamina] 3 [Action Die]

#### Zuva'Sai · Level 2 (p40)

> **Sidebar — Bold Strike card:** [Ally Slow] 1 [Stamina] [Melee Attack] + 2 [Action Die] +1 [Success]

##### Aquatic Jolt (Hydromancer) (p40)

Spend 3 stamina. Roll a magic attack using 3 [Action Die]. This attack targets one Enemy in each of the first 3 areas in a straight line of sight from your area.

> **Sidebar — Aquatic Jolt card:** [Ally Medium] 3 [Stamina] [Magic Attack] [Range] 1-3 [?icon: three filled hexagons in a diagonal line — straight line] 3 [Action Die]

##### Domination (Telepath) (p40)

Spend 3 stamina. Choose a non-Elite Enemy in line of sight within range 3. The chosen Enemy gains the Dominated condition.

> **Sidebar — Domination card:** [Ally Medium] 3 [Stamina] [Range] 3 Non-Elite: [Dominated]

#### Zuva'Sai · Level 3 (p40)

##### Eye of Serupod (p40)

A passive action. When you lock 2 stamina on this action, summon the Eye of Serupod token in line of sight within range 1. Once on each of your subsequent turns, the Eye may move 2.

Search: The Eye reveals Story cards and triggers compulsory effects on them as a Non-Hero Ally does, but it may not interact with them in any other way.

Channel: When performing any action that has a ranged effect, you may perform that action as if your Hero is in the same area as the Eye, but doing so ends this passive action.

*Illustration: the Eye of Serupod token.*

> **Sidebar — Eye of Serupod card:** [Ally Slow] [Lock Stamina] 2 [Range] 1 (the Eye of Serupod token): Move·2, Search, [Ends]: Channel [Magic Attack]

##### Rippling Image (Hydromancer) (p40)

A passive action. When you lock 2 stamina on this action, summon the Rippling Image token in line of sight within range 1. The Image has a taunt value of 10 and may be targeted by Enemies. If an Enemy attacks the Image, this passive action ends.

*Illustration: the Rippling Image token.*

> **Sidebar — Rippling Image card:** [Ally Fast] [Lock Stamina] 2 [Range] 1 (the Rippling Image token): Taunt 10 [Ends]: Enemy [Attack] (the Rippling Image token)
> <!-- ? the two small icons after "Enemy" in [Ends] are read as a generic Attack marker plus a repeat of the Rippling Image token -->

##### Psychic Link (Telepath) (p40)

A passive action. When you lock 1 stamina on this action, mark another Hero with the Psychic Link token. Once on each of the marked Hero's turns, they may recover 1 spent stamina.

*Illustration: the Psychic Link token.*

> **Sidebar — Psychic Link card:** [Ally Medium] [Lock Stamina] 1 Other Hero: (the Psychic Link token) [Restore Stamina] 1 /turn

#### Zuva'Sai · Level 4 (p40)

##### Cyclone (p40)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Push·3 all Enemies in line of sight within range 3.

> **Sidebar — Cyclone card:** [Ally Medium] [Inspiration Gem] [Range] 3 Push·3

##### Metamorphose (Hydromancer) (p40)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Mark a non-Elite Enemy in line of sight within range 3 with the Metamorphose token, Metamorphose symbol side up. The marked Enemy does not take a turn and moving out of its area does not provoke.

*Illustration: the Metamorphose token.*

At the start of your next turn, flip the Metamorphose token to its Zuva’Sai symbol side. At the start of your next turn after that, this effect ends. This effect also ends if the marked Enemy is defeated.

> **Sidebar — Metamorphose card:** [Ally Fast] [Inspiration Gem] [Range] 3 (the Metamorphose token) Non-Elite: No Turn or [Provoke] [Ends]: 2 Rounds

##### Mind Blast (Telepath) (p40)

Spend an Inspiration gem. Make a range 6 magic attack using 3 [Action Die]. An Enemy that takes damage from this attack gains the Stunned condition.

> **Sidebar — Mind Blast card:** [Ally Fast] [Inspiration Gem] [Magic Attack] [Range] 6 3 [Action Die] + [Stunned]
