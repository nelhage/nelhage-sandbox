### Hideouts (p43)

Your Hero may gain a hideout. If they do, during the City step you may place your Action Selection marker on the corresponding location on the city map to avoid the Disturbed Sleep penalty.

You may also stash an item or 10 Stars in the hideout. See Stashing Items and Stars on p. 9.

### Items (p43)

#### Item Cards (p43)

On the back of each Item card is a number and an image. The number is used to uniquely identify the Item card, while the image shows what type of item it is. The number of the Item card is also shown on the front of the card along with an individual number and count of those cards.
