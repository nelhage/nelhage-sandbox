> *The back of a Ranged Weapon Item card:*

> *The back of a Wand or Staff Item card:*
>
> *Card back illustration, numbered 013.*

| Effect | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [Melee Attack] | May be used as a melee attack weapon. |
| [Ranged Attack] | May be used as a ranged attack weapon. |
| [Magic Attack] | May be used as a magic attack weapon. |
| -1 [Success] /+2 [Success] | Modifies the number of successes rolled in the attack, i.e. the amount of damage the attack causes. |
| [Range] 0-1 / [Range] 1-2 | Ranged attack weapons and some melee attack weapons have a minimum and maximum range they may be used at. |
| [Weapon Rune] | The weapon may have one weapon rune Item card attached to it for each rune icon shown.<br>The weapon rune cards show this icon in their leftmost effect box. |
| [Arrow] | One arrow Item card may be used when you attack with the weapon.<br>The arrow cards show this icon in their leftmost effect box. |
| [Poison] | One poison Item card or Item token may be used when you attack with the weapon.<br>The poison cards show this icon in their leftmost effect box.<br>See Item Token Descriptions on p. 55 for a description of each poison Item token. |
| [Gem Slot] | One Item token showing the gem slot icon [Gem Slot] may be placed on the weapon for each gem slot icon shown. See Item Token Descriptions on p. 55.<br>You may either place an Item token onto the weapon during scenario setup, or during a scenario. To place an Item token onto the weapon during a scenario you must spend a maneuver. Place the Item token effect side up.<br>The effect of the Item token lasts until the end of the scenario. At the end of the scenario, discard the Item token.<br>You may place an Item token onto a weapon that already has its maximum number of tokens on it. If you do, discard one of the tokens on it. |

#### [Weapon Rune] [Arrow] [Poison] Weapon Runes, Arrows, and Poisons (p46)

>
> *The back of a Rune Item card:*
>
> *Card back illustration, numbered 054.*
>
> *The back of an Arrow Item card:*
>
> *Card back illustration, numbered 060.*
>
> *The back of a Poison Item card:*
>
> *Card back illustration, numbered 065.*

You may never have an unattached weapon rune Item card. Place each weapon rune card overlapping the weapon card it is attached to. When you store your items in your Hero board, ensure each weapon rune card is stored directly above the weapon card it is attached to. A weapon rune card may not be sold, but may be discarded at any time for no effect.

> **Remember:** Unless indicated otherwise, if the weapon rune, poison, or arrow is discarded when it is used you must discard it before you roll the [Action Die] for your attack to gain its benefit.

| Card | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **054 Rune of the Snake** | Discard at the start of the Ally and Enemy Turns phase to take your turn first this round. See Special Initiatives on p. 29. |
| **055 Rune of the Beast** | Discard after your attack roll to allow each [Action Die: Stamina] rolled to be used to add 2 successes to your roll. If you do, you may not use those dice for any other purpose. |
| **056 Rune of the Spider** | The weapon this is attached to has Pierce 1. See Pierce on p. 29.<br>Discard after your attack roll to roll 2 extra [Action Die] and add them to your attack. You still get Pierce 1 on this attack. |
| **060 Keeneye Arrow** | Discard to increase the maximum range of your ranged attack by 2 and gain 1 extra success on that attack. |
| **061 Corrosive Arrow** | Discard to gain 2 extra successes on your ranged attack. An Enemy that takes damage from this attack gains the Bleeding condition. |
| **062 Flame Arrow** | Discard to make your ranged attack an area 0 attack and roll 1 extra [Action Die]. See Area Attacks on p. 33. |
| **063 Frost Arrow** | Discard to make a non-Elite Enemy gain the Stunned condition if they take damage from your ranged attack. |
| **064 Heartseeker Arrow** | Discard to defeat a non-Elite Enemy targeted by your ranged attack. |
| **065 Snakeroot Sap** | Discard to gain 3 extra successes on your attack. |
| **066 Benaethid Bane** | Discard to gain 2 extra successes on your attack. An Enemy that takes damage from this attack gains the Weakened condition. |
| **067 Nazag Venom** | Discard to make a non-Elite Enemy gain the Stunned condition if they take damage from your attack. |
| **068 Void Extract** | Discard to defeat a non-Elite Enemy targeted by your attack. |
