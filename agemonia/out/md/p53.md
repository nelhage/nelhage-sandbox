#### Additional Item Descriptions and Clarifications (p49)

> **Sidebar — Item card backs:** *Card back illustrations, stacked down the left margin, for items 003, 012, 021, 035, 040, and 049.*

| Card | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **003 Pickaxe** | Does not count as an axe. |
| **006 Hook & Rope** | May be used as either a melee attack weapon that can attack up to range 1 or a ranged attack weapon that can attack from range 1-2. In either case, subtract 1 success from your attack roll.<br>**Climb** May be used to climb with.<br>*Illustration: a grapnel and coiled rope, captioned "Hook & Rope token".*<br>**A Hero with a Hook and Rope who is in the higher of two areas separated by a double white line showing a height symbol may:**<br>[Hook & Rope] » Discard the Hook & Rope » Place a Hook & Rope token on that double white line.<br>**A Hero with a Hook and Rope who is in the lower of two areas separated by a double white line showing a height symbol may:**<br>[Hook & Rope] » [Agility] 5 **Success:** Discard the Hook & Rope » Place a Hook & Rope token on that double white line.<br>**Fail:** You may spend another maneuver to try again this turn.<br>**An Ally in an area with a Hook & Rope token on one of its borders may spend 1 movement point to:**<br>[Agility] 4 **Success:** Move into the area on the other side of that border.<br>**Fail:** You may spend another movement point to try again this turn.<br>**A Hero in an area with a Hook & Rope token on one of it's borders may discard the Hook & Rope token to gain Item card 006 Hook & Rope.** |
| **012 Spear** | May be used as a melee attack weapon that can attack up to range 1. |
| **021 Buckler** | If you are attacked by an Enemy within range 1 and you take no damage from that attack, that Enemy suffers 1 damage. |
| **035 Fleetfoot Elixir** | Discard to Move 4. |
| **039 Draught of Veils** | Discard to gain the Invisible condition (see p. 58). |
| **040 Scroll of Telekinesis** | Spend a maneuver. Discard to move an item within range 3 to another area within range 3. If the item is being carried by a Hero, they must agree to the item being moved. If there is a Hero in the area the item is moved to, you may give the item to that Hero. |
| **042 Scroll of Lethargy** | Spend a maneuver. Discard to make an Enemy within range 1-3 who has not already had their turn this round to take their turn last this round. Place the “Last” Special Initiative token next to the Enemy’s Health Counter die to remind you. |
| **045 Scroll of Banishment** | Spend a maneuver. Discard to defeat an Aox, non-Elite Enemy within range 0-2. |
| **047 Scroll of Summoning** | Spend a maneuver. Discard to summon the appropriate Summoned Ally at range 0. See Summoned Allies on p. 42. |
| **049 Cube of Teleportation** | Discard to Teleport 5. See Teleport on p. 30. |
| **050 Cube of Annihilation** | Spend a maneuver. Discard to make an area 1 magic attack using 3 [Action Die]. Each [Action Die: Stamina] you roll may be used to add 2 successes to your roll. You may not spend stamina to increase this further. |
| **051 Prismatic Jewel** | Discard to gain an extra maneuver this turn. |
| **052 Jade Figurine** | Spend a maneuver. Discard to summon a Togrel at range 0-1. See Summoned Allies on p. 42. |
| **053 Obsidian Figurine** | Spend a maneuver. Discard to summon a Vespir at range 0-1. See Summoned Allies on p. 42. |
| **074 Chest of Relocation** | Always bought and sold as a pair.<br>Spend a maneuver to teleport an encumbrance 0 item to the Hero with the other Chest of Relocation. |
