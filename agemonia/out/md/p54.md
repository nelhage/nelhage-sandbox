| Card | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **075 Aox Scimitar** | Spend 1 stamina each time you perform a melee attack with this weapon. Gain 2 extra successes on this attack. |
| **076 Glacial Fang** | Gain 2 extra successes on your melee attack. For each [Action Die: Extra Roll] you roll, a non-Elite Enemy that takes damage from your attack gains the Stunned condition. This does not use the die, so you may still use that die to get 1 [Success] and 1 extra roll. |
| **077 Runeblade** | Gain 2 extra successes on your melee attack.<br>For each weapon rune you discard during your attack, roll a [Reaction Die]. If you roll [Reaction Die: 1 Shield], do not discard the weapon rune. You may not use the same weapon rune more than once during the same attack. |
| **078 Voidrender** | Gain 2 extra successes on your melee attack. For each Enemy you defeat during this attack, restore 1 spent stamina. |
| **079 Verdant Stinger** | Gain 1 extra success on your melee attack. For each [Action Die: Extra Roll] you roll, an Enemy that takes damage from your attack gains the Bleeding condition. This does not use the die, so you may still use that die to get 1 [Success] and 1 extra roll. |
| **080 Quothian Dirk** | Get Pierce 1 on your melee attack. See Pierce on p. 29.<br>You may spend up to 3 stamina instead of 2 for each [Action Die: Stamina] you roll during this attack to gain an equal number of successes. If you do, you may not use that die for any other purpose. |
| **081 Tear of Eti** | Get Pierce 1 on your melee attack. See Pierce on p. 29.<br>You may spend 3 stamina at the start of the Ally and Enemy Turns phase to take your turn first this round. See Special Initiatives on p. 29. |
| **082 Ceremonial Spear** | May be used as a melee attack weapon that can attack up to range 1. Gain 1 extra success and Pierce 1 on your melee attack. See Pierce on p. 29. |
| **083 Compendium Aoxa** | If you have this item as an active item, you may use the following 2 effects:<br>Each Blood Crystal token you discard during a magic attack allows you to roll 2 extra [Action Die] instead of the normal 1.<br>For each [Action Die: Stamina] you roll during a magic attack, you may suffer 1 damage to gain 2 successes instead of spending 2 stamina. If you do, you may not use that die for any other purpose.<br>**Note:** The Compendium Aoxa does not count as a weapon, so you may also use another item as a magic weapon. |
| **084 Moonsilver Cane** | Gain 1 extra success on your magic attack. You may spend 5 stamina after your attack to gain the Invisible condition. |
| **085 Staff of Ata Cahay** | Increase the range of your magic attack by 1. Gain 2 extra successes on your magic attack. |
| **086 Vyrgon´s Crook** | Gain 2 extra successes on your magic attack. This magic attack costs 1 fewer stamina. |
| **087 Wand of Beasts** | Gain 1 extra success and Pierce 1 on your magic attack. See Pierce on p. 29.<br>For each [Action Die: Extra Roll] you roll during this attack, a Beast Enemy that takes damage from the attack gains the Held condition. This does not use the die, so you may still use that die to get 1 [Success] and 1 extra roll. |
| **088 Wand of Geomancy** | Gain 1 extra success on your magic attack. For each Enemy that takes damage from this attack you may spend 1 stamina to give that Enemy the Slowed condition. |
| **089 Wyldkin Wand** | Increase the range of your magic attack by 1. Roll 1 extra [Action Die] during this attack. After this attack, gain the Cursed condition. |
| **090 Elakira’s Sliver** | Gain 1 extra success on your magic attack. If you used a level 1 or 2 Magic attack, it costs 1 fewer stamina. |
| **091 Nafabi Longbow** | Gain 1 extra success and Pierce 1 on your ranged attack. See Pierce on p. 29.<br>If you discard an arrow during your attack, roll a [Reaction Die]. If you roll [Reaction Die: 1 Shield], do not discard the arrow. |
| **092 Longbow of Grenak** | Gain 2 extra successes on your ranged attack. |
