#### Item Token Descriptions (p55)

Note that some Item tokens have no effect. These may be used by one or more professions or simply sold for Star.

##### [Poison] Poisons (p55)

Item tokens that are poisons show the poison icon [Poison] and must be used with a weapon card that also shows that icon. See Common Weapon Effects on p. 46.

##### [Gem Slot] Gem Slots (p55)

Item tokens that can be slotted into a gem slot show the gem slot icon [Gem Slot] and must be used with a weapon that also shows that icon. See Common Weapon Effects on p. 46.
