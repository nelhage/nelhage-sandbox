### Injury cards (p59)

*Illustration: the back of an Injury card, showing a red-gold insignia with a blood drop.*

| Name | Effect | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Battered | [Melee Attack] -1 [Success] | Get one fewer success when performing a melee attack. |
| Injured Eye | [Ranged Attack] [Magic Attack] -1 [Success] | Get one fewer success when performing a ranged or magic attack. |
| Pulled Back / Broken Ribs / Cracked Skull | [Might] -1 [Agility] -1 [Will] -1 | Treat your corresponding ability level as being 1 lower. |
| Winded | [Might] [Agility] [Will] -1 | Get one fewer success when performing an active ability check. |
| Injured Ankle | -1 [Movement Point] | Get one fewer movement point when you perform a Move maneuver. |
| Strained | Recover: [Restore Stamina] -1 | Restore one fewer stamina when you perform a Recover maneuver. |
| Lacerated | Recover: [Heal] -1 | Heal one fewer damage when you perform a Recover maneuver. |
| Injured Elbow | -1 [Hand] | You have one fewer hands for your active items. If necessary, immediately move one of your active items into your backpack area. If this exceeds your encumbrance limit (see p. 23), you must drop items in your area until you are within this limit. |
| Injured Mouth | May not [?icon: black circle with a white X, "may not use"] Potion, Elixir, Draught, or Herb items. | You may not use Potion, Elixir, Draught, or Herb items. |
| Dazed | Initiative: [Last] | You always take your turn last each round. |
| Concussion | Actions and Story cards: -1 [Range] | All of your ranged attacks, magic attacks, and other actions with a range have their maximum range reduced by 1. The range you can reveal and interact with Story cards is reduced by 1. |
| Enfeebled | [Action Die: Stamina]: 0-1 [Success] | You may only spend 0 or 1 [Stamina] on each [Action Die: Stamina] you roll. |

### Trap cards (p59)

*Illustration: the back of a Trap card, showing a blue-green-and-gold insignia.*

There are 6 Trap cards, described below. Each is an area attack.

| Name | Each Affected Ally: | Each Affected Enemy: |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arcane Blast | Suffer 5 stamina loss reduced by a reactive Will check. Non-Hero Allies take this stamina loss as damage as normal. | If Non-Elite, gain the Stun condition. |
| Fire Burst | Suffer 5 damage reduced by a reactive Agility check. | Suffer 3 damage. |
| Nerve Gas | Roll an active Might check. If you fail, gain the Held condition. | Gain the Held condition. |
| Poison Needles | Suffer 4 damage reduced by a reactive Agility check. If you take damage, gain the Weakened condition. | Suffer 2 damage and gain the Weakened condition. |
| Psychic Cage | Roll an active Will check. If you fail, gain the Confused condition. | If Non-Elite, gain the Confused condition. |
| Razor Blades | Suffer 5 damage reduced by a reactive Might check. If you take damage, gain the Bleeding condition. | Suffer 2 damage and gain the Bleeding condition. |
