### Artifact cards (p60)

From level 3 onwards Artifact cards will become available to buy.

Each Artifact card shows several stages, each with a diamond shaped slot on the left hand side. If an Artifact card shows stages with numbered slots, those stages must be completed in order from top to bottom. If an Artifact card shows stages with slots without a number on them, the stages will have requirements which will determine the stage you may attempt to complete.

Each Artifact card may only have one of its stages attempted per City step and each stage is attempted by a single Hero. Each stage will direct you to read an entry in the Story book.

*Illustration: an Artifact card, its top tab numbered `01`.*

Once a stage has been successfully completed you will be told to place a Progress sticker [Progress Sticker] on the slot. Once you have successfully completed an Artifact card, you will gain the corresponding Item card.

### Difficulty Level (p60)

You may play each scenario at any difficulty level you like. There are 15 cards in the Initiative deck when playing on Normal difficulty level and 16 when playing on any other difficulty level.

To play on Normal difficulty level, use all of the Initiative cards with blue borders on the front.

*Illustration: an Initiative card back.*

#### Easy (p60)

To play on Easy difficulty level, use all of the Initiative cards with blue borders on the front and the Initiative card with a green border on the front.

#### Hard (p60)

To play on Hard difficulty level, use all of the Initiative cards with blue borders on the front and the Initiative card with a red border on the front that shows “Shuffle” (you will now have 2 Initiative cards that shuffle the Initiative deck).

#### Very Hard (p60)

To play on Very Hard difficulty level, use all of the Initiative cards with blue borders on the front except the one that shows “Shuffle”, and both of the Initiative cards with a red border on the front.

### Exploration Mode (p60)

If you prefer to have a little more time to explore scenarios with timers, you may play in Exploration Mode.

In Exploration Mode, whenever you reset a timer, remove all Fate card from the Timer pile and place them in the Fate card discard pile, including the most recent Fate card. The new timer count will start at zero.

You may play in Exploration Mode with any difficulty level.

### Component Limits (p60)

#### Items (p60)

If you gain an item and it is not in the Stock or the Archive (because all copies of it are already in use), instead gain an amount of Star equal to the sale price of the item.

#### Enemy Figures (p60)

If you need to spawn an Enemy and there are no more figures available, the Enemy is not spawned. If several Enemies are spawned at the same time and figures are only available for some of them, place the Enemies that are at the shortest range from an Ally first.

#### Condition cards / tokens (p60)

In the rare case that an Enemy gains a condition and there are no more cards or tokens available for that condition, they do not gain the condition. If an Ally would gain a condition, there are no more cards or tokens for that condition, but there is an Enemy with that condition, the Enemy loses the condition and the Ally gains the condition. Prioritize Heroes having a condition over Non-Hero Allies having a condition in a similar way. Remember, you can use the Agemonia App to keep track of Enemy Health and conditions.

> **Condition card / token priority:** Hero > Non-Hero Ally > Enemy

#### Coins (p60)

In the rare case that you run out of coins, record each Hero’s [Star] on their Hero sheet.
